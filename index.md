## Experience

### ACT Education Corp

#### Senior Research Scientist (2021 - Present)

- Advancing the research and development of the automated essay scoring engine (CRASE), which is actively used in multiple high-stakes writing assessments. With CRASE, we could save ~$1.5M annually in human scoring costs. My contributions include:
  - Developing and deploying multiple ML modules, such as main scoring engine, single-score scoring logic, a disturbing content classifier, and an off-topic detector.
  - Researching data efficiency (e.g., using synthetic data), novel model architectures (e.g., contrastive learning, ordinal scoring objectives), and methods for improving operational efficiency in automated scoring (e.g., confidence scoring).
  - Maintaining and improving existing ML modules in production.
- Researching ML models for predicting item characteristics of passage-based, multiple-choice questions, specifically focusing on models that can predict item-response theory (IRT) compatible results.
- Designing and prototyping LLM-based tools for content developers and students, including passage-based question generation and essay tutoring applications.
- Developing various internal automation solutions, including form assembly, sales region recommendation, open-ended survey response analysis, and question-solving-step generation.
